Privacy Policy — ViewId

Last updated: October 14, 2026

ViewId (package com.denirodev.ViewId) shows labels for nearby places over your camera view. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

  • ViewId has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We operate no servers that receive your data. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.
  • Some features contact third-party services (listed below). Those services receive technical information such as your IP address, and may be able to infer roughly where you are, as described.

Information the app uses

  • Location (precise and approximate). Used only while the app is open, on your device, to work out which places lie around you and in which direction. It is not stored by us and is not sent to us. To find which country (and, for some large countries, which region) you are in, the app asks Android's built-in geocoding service, which on most devices is provided by Google; your coordinates are sent to that service for this purpose. The app never uses location in the background.
  • Camera. Shows the live view that labels are drawn on. Camera frames are not recorded or uploaded. If you tap Share, the app creates a screenshot of the current view (which can include the camera image), keeps it temporarily in the app's private cache, and hands it to the app you choose. We do not upload it.
  • Purchases. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The app only learns whether you have purchased, and uses the install date provided by Android to time the free trial. We never see your payment details.
  • Feedback. Send Feedback opens your email app with a draft addressed to us. The draft may include technical details (startup timings and, if the app crashed, a crash log showing the error and code locations). Nothing is sent unless you send the email; if you do, we receive your email address and message.
  • Stored on your device. Your settings (language, units, distance, selected categories), downloaded place data, terrain tiles, purchase status and the last crash log. If Android backup is enabled on your device, app settings may be included in your Google account backup under Google's policies.

Third-party services the app contacts

  • Google Play services and Google Play — location provider and in-app purchases.
  • Android geocoding (provided by Google) — receives your coordinates to return your country/region.
  • GitHub (github.com, api.github.com) — hosts the place data. The app downloads files named by country or region code, so GitHub receives your IP address and the country/region you requested.
  • Amazon Web Services (S3 terrain tiles) — the app downloads elevation tiles identified by a map grid square of about 1° (roughly 100 km) around you, so AWS receives your IP address and that grid square.
  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia — when you open a place's details, the app asks Wikipedia for that place's summary using the place's coordinates or name (not your location). Wikimedia receives your IP address.
  • Maps — if you tap Navigate, the place's coordinates are opened in your maps app or in Google Maps in your browser.

These services handle data under their own privacy policies, and we do not control them. All downloads use HTTPS.

Children

ViewId does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or social features. The app shows a safety warning each time it starts, reminding users to be aware of their surroundings and that children should use it with parental supervision.

Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with third parties, because we do not receive it. Google Play may provide us with anonymous crash and performance reports collected by Android from users who have chosen to share diagnostics.

Retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you on any server. Data stored on your device is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. If you emailed us and want your message deleted, write to the address below.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question or request about your privacy, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and its date.
